WHAT DRIVES US

OUR MISSION

JNJ INTEGRATED SYSTEMS CO. mission is to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of the Information Technology requirements of our small to medium sized enterprises client base, which is by providing nationwide leadership, oversight, products and services for activities related to information technology needs.

OUR VISION

To be recognized as a leader for optimizing SME's client base investments in information technology through collaboration, partnership, strategic planning and products and services development and delivery. To build a long-lasting working relation with our clientele and business partners through the quality of our services.

OUR GOALS

Deliver more effective and efficient solutions by optimizing the return on information technology investment, finding and delivering on opportunities for technology enhanced business processes, leading appropriate consolidation and unification of technology solutions, and fostering the creation of collaborative applications across client base.

Be the provider of choice for our client base and future clients on technology by identifying, procuring, providing and supporting (where appropriate) reliable, secure, optimally performing products, services, and infrastructure that encourage the use of common technology.

Build a customer responsive organization that inspires confidence, is helpful, and actively collaborates to find enterprise-wide solutions while providing leadership in IT.

We will proactively identify, adapt and apply technology that will allow us to ensure delivery of quality products and services that we offer to our clients.
